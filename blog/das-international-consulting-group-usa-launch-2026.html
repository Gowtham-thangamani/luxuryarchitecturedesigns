DAS and Partners Expands to the USA

A leading UAE architecture and engineering design firm goes global — DAS International Consulting Group LLC launches in Cheyenne, Wyoming, bringing design and multi-disciplinary engineering expertise to the US market.

In a landmark step for one of the region's most recognised design and engineering practices, DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy — a leading UAE architecture and engineering firm — has announced its first international expansion. The company has established a new United States entity, DAS International Consulting Group LLC, carrying its architecture, interior design and multi-disciplinary engineering expertise into the American market.

The move formalises DAS and Partners' evolution from a UAE-headquartered practice into an international design and engineering group, with a dedicated US base and a standalone presence at dasinternationalconsulting.com.

A New Chapter: UAE Design Expertise Enters the US Market

Across Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah, DAS and Partners has built a reputation for design excellence delivered within an integrated practice — architecture and interior design coordinated seamlessly with structural, MEP, civil and BIM disciplines. From luxury residences and cultural-district projects to hospitality and mixed-use developments, the firm has refined a design language that balances regional identity with global ambition. DAS International Consulting Group LLC extends that same integrated philosophy to a new geography, giving American developers and owners access to a studio schooled in one of the world's most design-conscious markets.

Introducing DAS International Consulting Group LLC

The new entity operates as the US arm of the DAS design and engineering network, structured to serve clients across the American market while drawing on the parent firm's regional experience and technical bench.

DAS International Consulting Group LLC — At a Glance

Entity: DAS International Consulting Group LLC
US Office: 1712 Pioneer Ave, Suite 101, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States
Website: dasinternationalconsulting.com

What the US Launch Means for Architecture & Design

DAS International Consulting Group LLC brings a distinctly cross-disciplinary studio model to a market where design quality, sustainability and buildability increasingly go hand in hand. The group's core competencies — refined across the UAE portfolio — travel well to American briefs:

  • Architecture & interior design — concept through detailed design for residential, commercial, hospitality and institutional projects.
  • Integrated engineering — structural, MEP and civil engineering coordinated alongside the architecture, reducing the interface risk that slows down complex projects.
  • Sustainable design — passive strategies, high-performance envelopes and energy modelling built into the design from concept stage.
  • BIM & digital delivery — model-based coordination, clash detection and documentation that support faster, cleaner delivery from design through construction.

"Great design is a universal language. Establishing DAS International Consulting Group LLC lets us bring the studio's integrated approach — architecture and engineering, working as one — to clients in the United States." — DAS and Partners

Bridging Two Markets

The transatlantic structure is designed to work in both directions. US clients gain a partner able to mobilise a deep, established design and engineering team and a proven delivery methodology; DAS and Partners, in turn, gains a platform to serve international clients and to bring global best practice back into its UAE studios. It is the kind of two-way exchange that sharpens a firm's creative and technical edge — and it signals real confidence in the DAS model as something that travels well beyond the region where it was built.

Looking Ahead

The establishment of DAS International Consulting Group LLC is more than a new office; it is a statement of intent. As DAS and Partners continues to shape the UAE's architectural landscape, its expansion into the United States positions the wider group for a new phase of growth — one that connects Gulf design excellence with North American opportunity. Luxury Architecture Designs will continue to follow the group's international progress and the projects that follow.
