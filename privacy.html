Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with us through this website.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

Luxury Architecture Designs is an editorial publication covering luxury architecture and design in the United Arab Emirates. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to luxuryarchitecturedesigns.com only, and not to any third-party website we link to.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit an enquiry or quote request, we collect the details you enter in the form — typically your name, email address, phone number, company and your message. When you subscribe to our newsletter we collect your email address, and your name if you provide it.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools record standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you visit and the site you arrived from. We use Google Analytics for this. It helps us understand which content is useful; it is not used to identify you personally.

How we use your information

We use the details you submit to respond to your enquiry, to prepare a quote or proposal, and to follow up about the project or topic you contacted us about. Enquiries submitted through this site are shared with DAS & Partners Engineering Consultancy, our affiliated engineering consultancy, so that the right specialist can reply to you. Newsletter addresses are used only to send the newsletter you asked for. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Legal basis and your consent

Where you submit a form, we process your details because you have asked us to respond to you. Where you subscribe to the newsletter, we process your email on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in any newsletter.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to deal with your request and to maintain a record of our correspondence, after which they are deleted or anonymised. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics to measure site usage. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to work normally without them.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, or ask us to delete it. You may also object to us using it, or ask us to stop contacting you. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you send us. No website can guarantee absolute security, so please do not send sensitive personal information, identification documents or payment details through our contact forms.

Children

This website is intended for a professional audience and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected here.

Contact us

If you have a question about this policy or about how we handle your information, please get in touch through our contact page.
